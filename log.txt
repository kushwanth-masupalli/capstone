Chest X-Ray Project - Log
======================================

Date Completed: August 30, 2026 (Yesterday)

What was done:
1. CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) applied to all raw chest X-ray images to enhance contrast.
2. All images resized to 224x224 pixels, matching DenseNet121 input dimensions.
3. Grayscale images converted to 3-channel RGB format for compatibility with pretrained models.
4. All 3,999 images successfully preprocessed and saved to data/images/preprocessed/ directory.
5. Preprocessing is idempotent — re-running the script skips already processed images.

Date Completed: August 31, 2026 (Today)

What was done:
1. Analyzed both CSV files (indiana_reports.csv and indiana_projections.csv) to understand structure and columns.
2. Merged both CSVs on 'uid' to link image filenames with diagnosis labels.
3. Filtered dataset to Frontal (PA) images only — removed Lateral views to avoid classifier learning view-dependent features instead of pathology.

- Total frontal images: 3,818 (out of 7,466)
- Unique patients: 3,689

- Top 15 labels: normal, Lung, Opacity, Calcinosis, Cardiomegaly, Pulmonary Atelectasis, Calcified Granuloma, Thoracic Vertebrae, Cicatrix, Spine, Markings, Aorta, Pleural Effusion, Diaphragm, Density
